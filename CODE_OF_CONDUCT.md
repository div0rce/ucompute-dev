# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, assume good faith, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks and sustained disruption are not tolerated in any project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, chat).

Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and comments that violate this, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.

Report conduct problems privately to the maintainers through the contact in `SECURITY.md`. Reports are handled confidentially.
