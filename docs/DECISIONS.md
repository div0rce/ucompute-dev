| D-019 | 2026-10-19 | **Unified compute credits with contributor consent**, a binding product and architectural principle (spec §26.5; economics model §29.4): "Contribute compute. Earn compute. Buy compute when you need more. Share your compute. Not your computer." One compute-credit economy, one consumer-facing balance and one interoperable network. Credits are earned when a contributor's authorized capacity serves funded workloads, including requests funded by purchased credits; purchased with ordinary currency once authorized; or granted from labelled, budgeted fair-access and promotional pools; and spent on supported network computation or Cloud. UCompute may charge a transparent platform fee per transaction. Each contributor controls participation, resource limits, accepted workloads, compensation terms (shown and confirmed before opt-in; re-confirmed when terms change) and availability for paid requests per device. Contributed capacity serves paid workloads only with that explicit consent; donated-only capacity is never sold or resold without separate informed authorization. Requesters see whether a request uses donated/free capacity or spends credits, and its price, before it runs; never silently charged. Owner: credits never require cryptocurrency or blockchain, and none is implemented at this stage; no production payment processing or provider cash payouts at this stage; plain usage billing may precede purchasable prepaid balances (spec §26.5 stages). Engineering guardrails (added by the 2026-10-19 engineering session to carry out the owner's "model the economics" and "no cash payouts at this stage"; they are not the owner's words and change only by recorded owner decision): (1) credits are issued only against a purchase or collected usage billing, or as labelled grants from a recorded, funded budget; earned credits are the (1 − f) share transferred from credits a requester spent, never new issuance, so every outstanding credit is backed; (2) no transfers between users and no cash redemption until the F-07-11 legal/compliance gates pass; (3) per-device sharing modes default off and are explicit: private/Mesh only, donate (free and fair-access requests only), share for credits (credit-funded requests on the owner's terms); (4) no production payments, payouts or real-money settlement without explicit owner authorization (spec §4, B-006). Free local Run, free private Mesh, self-hosted operation without UCompute-operated services or accounts, and open-source infrastructure are preserved; community access without payment remains possible through earned credits, donated capacity and fair-access mechanisms, subject to actual capacity; Cloud is an optional managed service on the same interoperable infrastructure, not a gateway; no competing credit currency or artificial network division. Credit value is a hypothesis until the §29.4 unit-economics model is supported by measurement. Scope: binds spec §§25–33, every implementation of the original rows and public materials; adds P1 row U-05 (M10); amends ADR-0004 decisions 3 and 7. Nothing of credits, payments, Swarm or Cloud is implemented; all stay disabled | Owner decision revision 2026-10-19; it replaces, before commit, the same-day draft ("Free Compute Commons") that barred community-contributed compute from paid work and so implied two economies. All 148 original rows and their evidence are preserved unchanged: F-07-02 (accounting), F-07-07 (credit ledger), F-07-08 (anti-Sybil), F-07-10 (price discovery), F-07-11 (gated settlement) and I-03 (reciprocity) now read as parts of one credit economy with consent-scoped contributor compensation. F-07-07's non-redeemable, no-monetary-value ledger and F-07-10/11's no-real-money criteria govern the current non-monetary stage; how F-07-07's statement is met truthfully once payments back credits needs a recorded owner decision before usage billing (§26.5). Settlement and payouts stay gated (F-07-11, B-006, §4). Per-device consent keeps "share your compute, not your computer"; backed issuance keeps earned credits a funded obligation; no transfer or cash-out limits stored-value and money-transmission exposure (not a legal opinion; counsel review under B-006). Consistent with spec §3 (no mandatory cryptocurrency), §9 (no redeemable value without compliance and fraud controls) and §23 (open core not a crippled demo) |
